Every workday now competes with a phone.

Personal devices are essential, and endlessly interruptive. Notifications, feeds, and apps pull attention away from the job in small moments that add up across a shift, a team, and a payroll.

Most employers are left with two poor options: ban phones outright, or look the other way. Neither works well for the business or its people.

How it works

Simple by design.

Clock Filter works alongside the time and payroll systems employers already use, so there is nothing new for employees to remember.

01

Clock in

When an employee clocks in, focus protection turns on automatically, following the employer’s policy.

02

Work with fewer interruptions

Employers set the rules, including breaks and flexibility, so the policy fits the workplace instead of the other way around.

03

Clock out

The moment the shift ends, focus protection turns off. The phone is fully the employee’s again.

Rewarding focus, not policing it.

Clock Filter is built around recognizing focused work. It gives employers a straightforward way to reward the people who make the most of their time on the clock.

Designed to work alongside leading payroll and time-and-attendance platforms.
Our principles

Built to be fair to employees.

Tools like this only work if people trust them. These principles guide every decision we make.

Only on the clock

Clock Filter is active during paid work time and steps aside when the shift ends. Personal time stays personal.

Your conversations stay yours

Clock Filter is designed without access to the content of personal messages or conversations.

Clear, not hidden

Employees know when Clock Filter is active and what it means for their workday.

Recognition over punishment

Our approach centers on rewarding focused work, and participation in reward programs is voluntary.
